How to Play

  1. Fill the grid with consecutive numbers, starting from 1.

  2. After selecting a cell, you must follow specific movement rules to choose the next cell:

    Either skip 2 cells when moving streight.

    Or skip 1 cell when moving diagonally.

  3. Click a valid cell to place the next number.

  4. The game ends when the grid is completely filled, or no valid moves remain.
